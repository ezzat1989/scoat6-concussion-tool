SCOAT6 Clinical Interpretation Note

SCOAT6 is not a standalone diagnostic test, but a multimodal clinical assessment. Athletes evaluated with ongoing symptoms, cognitive decrement, or balance impairment MUST NOT return to contact sports on the same day. Any return to learn / play should follow a graduated stepwise protocol supervised by a licensed healthcare professional.
